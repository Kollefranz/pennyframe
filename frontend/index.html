Connect to OpenRouter

Pennyframe makes images through OpenRouter. Connecting takes about a minute:

  1. Click the button below. OpenRouter opens in your browser.
  2. Sign in, or create a free account.
  3. Click Authorize, then come back here.

Your password never reaches Pennyframe. Images are billed by OpenRouter, so you'll need a little credit on your account: add credit.

Waiting for OpenRouter…
